The game

The easiest racket sport to pick up.
The hardest one to put down.

  1. 01
    Always doubles

    Two a side on a compact, walled court, so everyone is always in the rally.

  2. 02
    The walls are in play

    Let the ball bounce, then play it off the glass. It is where the best points happen.

  3. 03
    Underarm serve

    Bounce it, serve below the waist, land it diagonally. No booming serves to learn first.

  4. 04
    Tennis scoring

    Fifteen, thirty, forty, game. If you know tennis, you already know the score.
